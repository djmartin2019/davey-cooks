# Cast-Iron Ribeye with Sautéed Mushrooms

## Overview
Pan-seared ribeye finished with butter, whole garlic cloves, rosemary, and thyme, with sautéed mushrooms and mashed potatoes.

## Ingredients
- 2 ribeye steaks
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Avocado oil, for searing
- Unsalted butter, for basting
- Whole garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- Fresh rosemary and thyme
- Mushrooms, sliced
- Worcestershire sauce, for mushroom sauce
- Mashed potatoes, to serve (already on hand)

> Exact steak weights and mushroom-sauce ingredient quantities were not retained in the conversation; season and scale to your portions.

## Method
1. Pat steaks dry and season with salt and pepper.
2. Heat cast iron and add a thin layer of avocado oil. Sear one steak at a time if the pan cannot comfortably fit both.
3. Add butter, garlic, rosemary, and thyme after a crust forms. Tilt pan and spoon aromatic butter over steak.
4. Check internal temperature with a thermometer; transfer steak to rest. Cook the second steak separately, starting with fresh avocado oil as needed.
5. Reserve suitable flavorful drippings for the mushrooms. Sauté mushrooms until browned, then finish with a little Worcestershire and pan juices. Adjust salt and pepper.
6. Serve with mashed potatoes.

## Actual cooking notes
- First steak measured **140°F** when checked. It was held in the oven while the second steak cooked because one diner preferred more well-done beef; the final internal temperature was not documented.
- Butter was infused with rosemary, thyme, and garlic; the cooked garlic cloves were particularly delicious.
- Cook reported the steak dinner went very well.
- Two steaks were cooked sequentially because of skillet space.

## Food safety
Use a thermometer. USDA guidance for whole beef steaks is 145°F with a 3-minute rest; lower steak temperatures are a personal risk choice.
