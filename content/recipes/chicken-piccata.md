# Chicken Piccata with Angel Hair Pasta

## Overview
Thin flour-dredged chicken cutlets, pan-seared until golden and served with lemon-butter sauce, capers, parsley, Parmesan, and angel hair pasta. Garlic bread was served alongside in the finished meal.

## Ingredients
### Chicken
- 1–1 1/4 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 2 breasts, cut into 4 cutlets)
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour, for dredging (not all will be used)
- Salt and black pepper
- 2 tbsp avocado oil, plus more if needed

### Sauce
- 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tbsp drained capers **(omit in preferred future version)**
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tbsp cold unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley
- Optional: tender parsley stems, minced with garlic, or whole stems infused briefly and removed

### Serving
- 6–8 oz angel hair pasta
- Reserved pasta water, as needed
- Freshly grated Parmesan, optional
- Garlic bread, optional

## Method
1. Slice breasts horizontally into 4 cutlets and pound uneven sections to approximately 1/2 inch thick. Pat dry and season.
2. Lightly dredge cutlets in flour, shaking off excess.
3. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sear in batches, approximately 3–4 minutes first side and 2–3 minutes second side, until golden and **165°F** at the thickest point. Remove to a clean plate.
4. In the **same pan**, leave about 1 tbsp of drippings. Lower heat to medium and sauté minced garlic for about 30 seconds.
5. Deglaze with chicken broth, scraping up browned fond. Add lemon juice and capers (if using); simmer 2–3 minutes to reduce slightly.
6. Lower heat or remove pan from burner. Whisk in cold butter, a piece at a time, until glossy. Taste; dilute with a little extra broth if too sharp.
7. Cook angel hair in salted water according to package directions; reserve some cooking water.
8. Return chicken and resting juices to sauce briefly to warm. Toss pasta with sauce and a splash of pasta water if needed. Plate with chicken, parsley, Parmesan, and optional garlic bread.

## Actual cooking notes
- **Very successful dinner.** Chicken had excellent browning and remained juicy.
- Sauce tasted sharp before the butter; butter rounded it out, and an additional small splash of broth improved balance.
- Capers were the one disliked ingredient; preferred future version omits them.
- Finished plate included angel hair, parsley, grated Parmesan, and garlic bread.
- Pasta appeared lightly coated in the photo; next time reserve enough sauce to toss the noodles more thoroughly.

## Next time
- Make **lemon-garlic butter chicken** without capers, keeping the rest of the method.
- Optionally add a little lemon zest for aroma without substantially increasing acidity.
- Record the extra broth amount used during the final seasoning adjustment.
