# Cast-Iron Steakhouse Cheeseburgers

## Overview
Thick 80/20 ground-chuck burgers with melted cheese, caramelized onions, dill pickles, crisp lettuce, and homemade sauce on toasted buns.

## Ingredients
### Burgers
- 4 H-E-B 80/20 ground-chuck patties, **1/3 lb each** (1 1/3 lb total)
- Salt and black pepper
- Avocado oil, for skillet
- 4 brioche or potato buns
- 4 slices American cheese (or sharp cheddar)
- 1 large yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp butter, plus a little avocado oil, for onions
- Dill pickle chips
- Shredded iceberg lettuce

### Burger sauce
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tsp yellow mustard
- 1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp finely chopped dill pickles
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp smoked paprika

## Method
1. Cook sliced onion in butter and a little oil over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden (roughly 20–30 minutes). Add a splash of water if needed to prevent scorching.
2. Stir together burger sauce ingredients and refrigerate.
3. Pat patties dry, season their exteriors with salt and pepper, and gently indent centers if rounded.
4. Heat cast iron over medium-high with a thin film of avocado oil. Cook in batches without crowding or pressing the patties. Approximate starting point: 4–5 minutes per side, but **use internal temperature rather than time**.
5. Cook ground beef to **160°F**. Add cheese in the last minute and cover briefly to melt.
6. Toast buns cut-side down in a lightly greased skillet for 30–60 seconds.
7. Assemble with sauce, lettuce, pickles, cheeseburger, onions, and top bun.
