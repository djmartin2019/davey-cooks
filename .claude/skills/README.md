## Editorial conventions
- **Actual cooking notes** reflect the cook's reported results and available photos.
- **Proposed next version** is untested, not a record of what was cooked.
- Ingredient amounts missing from the discussion are explicitly marked rather than invented.
- No external recipe URLs were cited in the original cooking conversations.
- Follow safe handling practices and verify internal temperatures with a thermometer.
